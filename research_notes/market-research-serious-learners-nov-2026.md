## 3. How long it takes (verified)

- Cambridge English, *Guided learning hours* (helpdesk) and its 2013 CEFR guide:
  roughly 200 guided learning hours per CEFR step. Totals from the start:
  **A2 ≈ 180–200, B1 ≈ 350–400, B2 ≈ 500–600**. These are guidelines; hours
  grow as levels rise.
  https://support.cambridgeenglish.org/hc/en-gb/articles/202838506-Guided-learning-hours
- What this means for our rooms: up to 2 × 90-minute live classes a week is
  about 12 live hours a month. Live hours alone cannot move a full CEFR level in
  weeks. Daily practice between classes is not optional.

## 4. Why people quit (verified)

- Self-paced courses: in the Harvard/MIT edX data (Reich & Ruipérez-Valiente,
  *Science*, 2019), **52% of registrants never entered the course**, and low
  completion did not improve over six years. Among learners who *intended* to
  finish, about **1 in 5** earned a certificate (Reich 2014, HarvardX).
  https://tsl.mit.edu/research/the-mooc-pivot/ ·
  https://er.educause.edu/articles/2014/12/mooc-completion-and-retention-in-the-context-of-student-intent
- Adult language classes: learners leave over class experience (feeling
  ignored, a teacher who does not care), personal relevance and life load
  (fatigue, family), more than lack of interest in English (several studies; see
  sources in the search log). Implication: a named teacher, a small room, and a
  course tied to one real situation are what keep adults in class.

## 5. Why it matters for jobs (verified, with sample caveats)

- **NITI Aayog (Feb 2025), *Expanding Quality Higher Education through States
  and State Public Universities*:** names inadequate English proficiency as a
  barrier to employment for state-university graduates (state universities
  teach 80%+ of India's higher-education students).
  https://niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Expanding-Quality-Higher-Education-through-SPUs.pdf
- **ETS TOEIC Global English Skills Report (March 2026):** in India, 91% of HR
  decision makers say a more competitive job market raises the need for English
  proficiency (81% globally); 84% say weak English is a competitive
  disadvantage. **India sample: 87 HR decision makers** (always say so).
  https://www.ets.org/insights-and-perspectives/english-and-ai-india-career-formula.html
- **India Skills Report 2026:** national employability 56.35% (2025: 54.81%).
  No 2026 English-specific state figure found; do not invent one.
- **SPJIMR working paper WPR-2026-10 (June 2026),** "India Inc, AI, and the
  communication competence gap": frames weak communication in new managers as
  an execution risk for employers. No quantitative result confirmed, so cite
  the framing only.

## 6. BPO screening (verified)

- Versant and similar tests are used by large BPOs (for example Concentrix and
  Teleperformance for international voice) as an early screen. **There is no
  public, official India cut-off score**; the employer sets it. Do not print a
  "target Versant score" as fact.

## 7. AI apps vs live teachers (verified, small studies)

- 2025 studies: AI speaking tasks can lower speaking anxiety and help practice;
  none found that AI outperforms a live teacher; several conclude AI works best
  **alongside** teacher-led practice. Small samples, mostly university EFL
  students. Use as: "use an app for daily reps, not as the teacher".

## 8. Rules for our own claims (ASCI education guidelines)

- No "100%" claims for abstract outcomes (placement, job assistance).
- Every claim must be substantiable. No "No. 1", no rankings we did not earn.
- Do not portray learners who struggle as failures.
- Named outcomes on /success-stories only with consent; no "salary doubled"
  phrasing as a promise.
  https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ASCI-GUIDELINES-FOR-EDUCATIONAL-INSTITUTIONS-PROGRAMMES-AND-PLATFORMS.pdf

## 9. What this means for the site (the "serious learner" brief)

Every blog post and guide now ends with a course-fit check that:

1. States what the course demands: live slots, months, daily practice, total fee.
2. States who should not join (children, exam score seekers, 1:1 seekers,
   "fluent in 30 days" seekers, anyone who cannot attend live).
3. Ends with one direct WhatsApp button. Its prefilled message says the learner
   has read the commitment and is ready, so the chat opens on intent; the
   teacher asks for level, goal and slot in the reply.

The point is fewer, better WhatsApp chats: people who have read the commitment
and still want in.
